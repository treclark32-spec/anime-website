What Is Anime?

Anime is a style of animated entertainment that originated in Japan. It includes different types of stories, characters, and art styles. Anime can be made for people of many different ages.

Anime has become popular around the world because of its different style of storytelling and creative animation. Popular series such as Naruto, Dragon Ball, One Piece, and Attack on Titan have gained large audiences around the world.

Anime Around the World

Anime has fans all around the world and continues to grow in popularity.

Anime Genres

Anime covers many different genres. Some of the most common anime genres are action, adventure, comedy, fantasy, and science fiction.

  • Action
  • Adventure
  • Comedy
  • Fantasy
  • Science Fiction
