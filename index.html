History and Popularity of Anime

What Is Anime?

Anime is a style of animated entertainment that originated in Japan. It includes different types of stories, characters, and art styles. Anime can be made for anyone no matter the age.

Anime has become popular around the world because of its very diffrent style of storytelling and creative animation. Series that are realy good such as Naruto, Dragon Ball, One Piece, and Attack on Titan have gained large audiences around the world.
